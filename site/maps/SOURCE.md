Derived GeoJSON keeps original geometry and Korean display names. ISO_A2 is used when assigned; France and Norway use ISO_A2_EH. Northern Cyprus, Somaliland, Kosovo have no assigned ISO code in this schema and remain unmapped (not assigned to another country). This generalized 177-feature map omits small territories; the country selector covers all schema ISO codes. Boundaries follow Natural Earth's de facto representation and do not express a political position.
`nTaiwan is explicitly mapped to TW (assigned ISO 3166-1 code); the upstream political variant of ISO_A2 is not used.
